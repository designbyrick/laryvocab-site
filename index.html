Subscriptions

To restore an existing purchase, open Profile → Restore purchases in the app. To change or cancel your subscription, use Apple's subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Account and privacy

You can delete your LaryVocab account from Profile → Delete Account. For questions about your data, contact us by email or read our Privacy Policy.

Something not working?

Tell us what happened, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the app version if available. Do not email passwords, purchase receipts, or other sensitive information.
