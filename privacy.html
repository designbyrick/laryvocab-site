Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LaryVocab ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our iOS application.

By using LaryVocab, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve the app:

  • Account information: your email address and authentication credentials if you sign up with email, or your name and email provided by Apple if you use Sign in with Apple. You can also use the app with an anonymous account identifier.
  • Learning data: the words you study, quiz scores, streaks, accuracy, favorites, and bookmarks. This powers spaced repetition and progress tracking.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Pro subscription.
  • Feedback: messages and related word or game context that you deliberately send through the feedback form.
  • Local reminders: streak reminders are scheduled locally on your device after you grant notification permission. We do not store a push notification token for these reminders.
  • Development analytics: limited product events can be enabled in internal development builds to test onboarding and practice flows. Product analytics are disabled in production App Store builds.
  • Operational device information: our service providers may process limited app, device, and operating-system information needed for compatibility, subscription verification, security, and diagnostics.
  • Crash logs: collected automatically via Apple's diagnostics tools to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use this information to:

  • Provide and personalize your learning experience
  • Sync your progress across sessions
  • Send streak reminder notifications (only if you opt in)
  • Manage your subscription
  • Review feedback that you choose to submit
  • Monitor app performance and diagnose bugs

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase – secure cloud database for storing your account and learning data
  • RevenueCat – subscription and entitlement management
  • PostHog – privacy-limited analytics used only in internal development builds; production delivery, automatic capture, session replay, surveys, and location enrichment are disabled
  • Apple App Store & Sign in with Apple – authentication and in-app purchases
  • Apple Diagnostics – crash reporting and device analytics

These providers may collect anonymized data according to their own privacy practices. No personally identifiable information is intentionally shared beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab inside the app. Deleting your account permanently removes all your personal data and learning history from our servers.

If you wish to request deletion of any remaining data, contact us at gusman.design4@gmail.com and we will process your request promptly.

5. Children's Privacy

LaryVocab is rated 4+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware of unintentional data collection from a child, we will delete it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes go into effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
