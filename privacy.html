Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

LaryVocab ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use LaryVocab:

  • Account information: your email address and password (if you sign up with email), or your name and email provided by Apple (if you use Sign in with Apple).
  • Learning data: the words you study, your quiz scores, streaks, accuracy, favorites, and bookmarks. This data is used to power spaced repetition and track your progress.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Pro subscription, managed via RevenueCat.
  • Push notification token: if you grant notification permission, we store a token to send you streak reminders.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the app experience
  • To sync your progress across sessions
  • To send streak reminder notifications (only if you opt in)
  • To manage your subscription

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Subscription data is managed by RevenueCat. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab inside the app. Deleting your account permanently removes all your personal data and learning history from our servers.

Children's Privacy

LaryVocab is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
